# Tubes, stops and branches {#tubes-stops-and-branches}

## A compact tube network {#a-compact-tube-network}

Every tube occupies one block. Straights, corners, verticals and rising or descending elbows let you route around a build. Clear panels show the path; attached coloured rims make connections easy to follow.

![Teal tubes travelling horizontally and turning upward](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/teal-route.png)

**Teal, coral, gold, blue, violet and white** share the same connections and crafting costs. Colour is a building choice, not a restriction on where passengers can go.

## Two entrance shapes {#two-entrance-shapes}

The **Harbour Station** and **Overlook Station** use an elbow entrance with a downward mouth and sideways route port. The **Straight Sucker Station** sends the route upward. All are open, automatic intakes with a yellow-and-black mouth rim and a quiet local suction sound.

![Open warning-striped mouth on the white straight sucker](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/straight-intake.png)

Hang a mouth two blocks above a solid landing and leave room to stand beneath it. F names the stop; boarding needs no key press.

## Choose your destination {#choose-your-destination}

A three-way **Tube Splitter** connects more stops. Complete routes continue to work when an unused branch is open. If more than one destination is reachable, the intake offers a named list before departure.

![Blue and coral stations connected through a gold junction](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/coloured-stations.png)

## The passenger experience {#the-passenger-experience}

You shrink to about 30% size, with the camera facing the direction of travel. Tubes guide your movement; WASD cannot pull you out. The player grows back on the landing and regains normal controls. Interrupted trips retain a safe-return record for recovery on rejoin.

The [Pipe Workbench](../recipes.md) supplies all 54 tube and station variants. See [preview limits](../configuration.md) before building a larger network.
