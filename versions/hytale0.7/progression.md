# From two stops to a network {#from-two-stops-to-a-network}

Start with iron and copper at the standard Workbench. Make a Pipe Workbench, then craft two suckers and a short straight route between them. Try boarding in both directions before extending it.

Next, add corners or verticals to cross a wall or change height. Keep the full-size landing areas clear, even when tubes pass through a tight enclosure.

![A small network with a white vertical intake and two coloured destinations](/assets/hytale0.7/screenshots/network-overview.png)

Add a splitter and a third named stop to unlock destination choice. Use colours to distinguish branches or match the surrounding build; all six colours connect.

This preview has no tiers, power, fares or cargo. Its next useful goal is a reliable named route through your own test build. [Recipes](recipes.md) show the costs; [preview limits](configuration.md) explain the current bounds.
