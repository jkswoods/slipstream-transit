# Troubleshooting {#troubleshooting}

## The intake will not start {#the-intake-will-not-start}

Step at least **1.5 blocks away**, then approach the centre again. Joining a world or finishing a trip does not immediately re-arm the mouth. Stand on a solid floor two blocks below it and leave room for a full-size player.

Check every adjacent opening, both landings and the actual tube bore. Blocks beside a tube are supported; blocks through its bore or standing area prevent travel. Load the whole route by staying nearby. Every connected part must share a builder.

## There is no destination chooser {#there-is-no-destination-chooser}

A direct two-stop route departs automatically. The chooser appears only when more than one other stop is reachable. Add a splitter, connect another sucker and name it with **F**.

## F opens a name field {#f-opens-a-name-field}

That is expected. **Walk beneath the mouth to board.** F is for naming or opening the Pipe Workbench.

## I want to return during a trip {#i-want-to-return-during-a-trip}

Crouch or use `/stabort` to request a safe return to the boarding stop. WASD stays locked until the player reaches full size again. Keep that landing intact and clear so interrupted journeys can recover.

## A trip was interrupted {#a-trip-was-interrupted}

Rejoin the same world and allow the safe-return transition to finish. Keep the original landing loaded and clear. Avoid removing the mod, dismantling the landing or changing builds while a player is in transit.

## What is still experimental? {#what-is-still-experimental}

Use a test world. Real second-client multiplayer, survival ingredient deduction and final sound/comfort review are pending. The visible tube shell has a narrow physical brace and a separate full-block selection area; treat the tube network as transport rather than a solid building surface.

Before changing versions, save normally and keep the matching full world, players and JAR backup. [Installation guidance](installation.md)
