# Changes

## C7 — passenger controls and appearance

- Movement stays locked during capture, travel and growth, then restores after arrival, safe return or affected reconnect.
- Player skin and cosmetics persist through size changes.
- Item and colour-category icons display at their complete native atlas size.

## C6 — network workshop

- Quieter suction within four blocks and smooth shrink/grow transitions.
- Enclosed travel with centred scaled bounds and obstruction checks.
- Three-way splitters, named destinations and automatic destination selection.
- Placement/F naming, full-block selection and rotation snapping.
- Pipe Workbench, six colours, improved original textures and striped straight/elbow mouths.

Current compatibility and limits are listed in [installation](installation.md) and [preview limits](configuration.md).
